---
title: Saudi-Yemeni conflict heats up in the Middle East
date: 2026-10-08
until: 2026-10-12
---
Significant escalation on the Saudi-Yemeni front, with the Ansarallah group of Yemen attacking an airport in Riyadh and a Saudi Aramco refinery, causing Brent crude to spike as much as 5%.

There are also reports of the Pakistan Air Force conducting bombing raids in Yemen as part of the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement between Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan.
